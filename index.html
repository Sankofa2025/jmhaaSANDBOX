About Tiarra

A Fierce Advocate for Your Rights and Your Future.

Tiarra Thompson is a Partner Attorney at Sankofa Law Group, where she provides relentless, strategic representation for clients navigating complex civil litigation, criminal defense, and family law matters. With a deep commitment to justice and a meticulous approach to case preparation, Tiarra understands that legal challenges are not just matters of law, but deeply personal events that impact her clients' lives, livelihoods, and futures.

She approaches every case with a dual focus: aggressive advocacy in the courtroom and compassionate, clear communication with her clients. Whether she is dissecting the nuances of a breach-of-contract dispute, protecting a client's rights during a criminal investigation, or guiding a family through a sensitive transition, Tiarra leaves no stone unturned.

Guided by the Sankofa principle, Tiarra believes in thoroughly understanding the origins of a legal dispute to forge the strongest possible path forward. She is fiercely protective of her clients' rights and is dedicated to ensuring their voices are heard and their interests are vigorously defended.

Areas of Focus

Strategic Representation Across Multiple Fronts.

Criminal Defense

Tiarra provides responsive, protective counsel for individuals facing criminal allegations. From the earliest stages of an investigation through negotiations, hearings, and trial, she ensures her clients' rights are safeguarded and their futures are protected against the long-term consequences of a conviction.

Civil Litigation

In civil matters, Tiarra evaluates the facts, legal options, and risks to develop a practical, results-driven path forward. She handles commercial disputes, breach-of-contract claims, and personal injury matters with a sharp analytical mind and a commitment to securing favorable outcomes.

Family Law

Navigating family law requires both legal acumen and deep empathy. Tiarra advocates fiercely for her clients in matters involving custody, support, and divorce, ensuring their rights and their children's well-being are prioritized during highly sensitive transitions.
