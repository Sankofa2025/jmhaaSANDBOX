$10M Verdict in Negligent Security Case

By L. Chris Stewart, Managing Partner 5 min read

A Fulton County jury has returned a landmark $10 million verdict in favor of our client, holding a major apartment complex accountable for failing to provide adequate security measures on its premises.

The case centered on a violent incident that occurred in the common areas of the apartment complex. Despite numerous prior incidents and complaints from residents, the property owners failed to implement basic security protocols that could have prevented the harm.

What the Jury Decided

After a two-week trial, the jury found that the property owners were negligent in their duty to protect residents and visitors from foreseeable criminal activity. The $10 million verdict included compensation for medical expenses, lost wages, and pain and suffering.

"This verdict sends a clear message to property owners across Georgia: you cannot ignore the safety of the people who live on and visit your premises."

What This Means for Victims

If you or a loved one has been injured due to inadequate security at an apartment complex, shopping center, or other property, you may have a claim for negligence. Property owners have a legal duty to provide reasonable security measures when criminal activity is foreseeable.
